# Short-form script review

Evaluate the opening promise, who it is for, why the next beat follows, and whether the payoff delivers. A feature-led hook or narrow audience can work when it matches the brief. Do not require a universal audience, suspense, or an answer withheld until the final frame.

For silent-feed distribution, inspect whether visuals and captions communicate the necessary context without sound. Use the supplied distribution requirements; do not apply a fixed 15-second test to every duration or audio-led film. If only a script is available, label visual, pacing, and sound observations as proposed checks rather than completed viewing.

Look for a broken promise between hook and body, disconnected shots, unreadable text at intended display size, and explanation that arrives before its context. Recommend a specific replacement or reorder and explain the likely benefit without guaranteeing views or retention. File integrity and audio/render checks belong with video editing; this review judges the content actually available.
