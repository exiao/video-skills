## Adapt the useful decisions

### First meaningful action

Find the first moment that confirms the post's promise. In a feed demo, remove empty setup when it contributes neither information nor anticipation. Start with visible work or a supported result and keep enough context to understand the task. Measure the actual idle interval rather than imposing a universal three-second cutoff. For a documentary, timing test, or reference recording, preserve latency when it is material.

### Frame the thing being compared

Give the working area enough screen space for the action and difference to read on a phone. Reduce irrelevant borders, chrome, and labels; retain essential subject identity, task, qualifiers, and evidence. Compare cropped and uncropped views at delivery size before choosing. Correct compression, grain, or washed-out capture at the source when possible; judge the actual export rather than prescribing saturation or sharpening globally.

### Distinguish presentation from measurement

Align stages or visible action when the goal is an illustrative comparison and make material time offsets or speed changes clear. If the clip claims speed, latency, fairness, or a winner, keep a common time origin and consistent playback speed, or display the real offsets and elapsed time. Do not independently trim thinking delays and then imply simultaneous starts or measured speed superiority. Preserve source recordings and identify edits in the production record. Synchronize for comprehension without falsifying the result.

### Make the challenge reveal something

When the concept is open, compare a straightforward capability test with an unusual task that exposes a meaningful difference, personality, or result. The self-portrait challenge makes viewers curious about both interpretations and the final builds. Prefer a question the outputs can visibly answer. Surprise, humor, or anthropomorphism is a framing choice; an output does not establish a model's inner experience or general capability ranking. A conventional task can still be right for a practical tutorial.

Use timely names only when relevant and supported by current audience evidence. Timeliness is a distribution hypothesis, not a one-day deadline or permission to rush past factual and playback checks. Preserve the user's model and tool preferences for actual production.

### Package and review at the encounter size

The post should identify the compared subjects and task quickly, with a short concrete prompt or result when useful. Inspect real mobile wrapping and the first visible video frame together. A desktop mockup cannot establish mobile layout. Avoid front-loading methodology details that obscure the reason to watch, while keeping consequential comparison qualifications accessible.

For a requested revision, record the change, source interval/frame, intended viewer benefit, and comparison facts that must remain true. Recheck the opening, the point where outputs diverge, and the payoff in actual playback. Distinguish technical improvement, editorial judgment, and observed audience outcomes; a before/after screenshot does not prove pacing or causal performance gains.
