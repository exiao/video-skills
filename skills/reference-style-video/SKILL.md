# Reference-style video

First distinguish style adaptation from recreation. “Like this” usually asks for transferable grammar; replacing a person or setting while retaining the performance calls for [reference-driven recreation](references/video-recreation.md). Honor explicit requests to preserve timing, actions, supplied audio, or other elements instead of silently converting them into a loosely inspired edit. Resolve only consequential missing choices; do not require a new intake when the request already defines them.

Measure before describing. Obtain the supplied reference or an authorized local copy, then inspect geometry, duration, cut timestamps, cuts per minute, locations, framing, caption placement, caption color, transitions, motion, sound, and the first two seconds as relevant. Use a contact sheet to navigate, then inspect the corresponding playback for motion and sound. Sampled frames alone do not prove timing, lip sync, acting, or audio fidelity. Never characterize the style from thumbnails, memory, or transcript alone.

Turn measurements into a beat sheet with one row per cut or distinct edit event: time, shot/framing, spoken or on-screen text, caption treatment, motion/transition, overlays, and audio. For style adaptation, summarize three to five signature moves to apply to the user's own footage and message; default to new content rather than copying the source's script, footage, music, or branding. For recreation, record the requested preservation and replacement choices and compare against those explicit requirements.

Build the new edit from the beat sheet, then measure it the same way. Compare cuts per minute, shot variety, caption readability, placement, and pacing; recreation also needs aligned shot and action checks. Report measured matches and remaining gaps plainly. This skill analyzes and directs; use [$video-script](../video-script/SKILL.md) for scripting, [$video-direction](../video-direction/SKILL.md) for generation handoffs, and [$video-editor](../video-editor/SKILL.md) for local ffmpeg operations. Use $yt-dlp (external resource; not included) when retrieving the supplied clip is needed for the requested analysis or recreation. Do not expand retrieval into unrelated uploads or playlists. Analysis does not authorize media generation, publishing, or purchases.

Read [references/measure-a-reference.md](references/measure-a-reference.md) for reproducible probes and [references/edit-anatomy.md](references/edit-anatomy.md) for the beat-sheet method. For animated typed captions, read [references/typed-captions.md](references/typed-captions.md).

For vertical talking-head placement, read [the Reels composition reference](references/reels-placement.md) and inspect its saved keyframes. Distinguish subtitle placement, title placement, eyeline, and platform-interface clearance; keep example coordinates separate from current platform specifications.
