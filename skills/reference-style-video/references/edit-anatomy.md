# Edit anatomy

Create a beat sheet with one row per cut or distinct event:

```text
beat | time | shot/framing | spoken/on-screen text | caption style |
motion/transition | overlay | audio
```

For style adaptation, extract patterns rather than copying every timestamp: hard cut plus punch-in per sentence, a word-pop caption treatment, a whoosh on transitions, or a silence before the close. For [recreation](video-recreation.md), retain the requested exact cut boundaries and action beats instead. Apply the appropriate constraints to the user's footage and message. Location variety and cut-on-the-line usually affect energy more than decorative camera moves. Full-bleed framing, readable captions, and deliberate black cards should be choices supported by the measured reference.

Style adaptation uses the reference's editing grammar with new content. Recreation follows the user's explicit keep/replace choices, using supplied or appropriately licensed media; do not assume a reference link alone requests reuse of its speaker, voice, music, or branding.
