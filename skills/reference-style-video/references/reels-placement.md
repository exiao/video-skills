## Apply the distinctions

- **Composition:** For a conventional talking head, start with eyes around the upper third and deliberate headroom. Preserve purposeful alternative framing. If footage is already low in frame, use its available negative space for text or reframe where the source permits; do not cover the face merely to maintain a fixed caption coordinate.
- **Subtitle versus title:** Place ongoing speech captions in a readable band below the face when that space is clear. Give the title hook a separate position above the head, inset from the top. Do not interpret “put text near the top” as a rule for every subtitle or graphic.
- **Safe area versus subject clearance:** A rectangle can be inside the safe area and still cover eyes, mouth, hands, or a product demonstration. Check both interface clearance and the meaning-bearing parts of the shot. Keep graphics and CTAs clear too.

Use labels or symbols alongside color so red/green alone does not carry the meaning. Adapt the explanatory technique without copying the creator's footage or expression into new work.
