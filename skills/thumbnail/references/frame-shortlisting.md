# Shortlist frames before choosing a cover

Use when long footage contains many plausible frames. The shortlist reduces review
effort; it does not decide the finished thumbnail or establish click-through rate.

Start with the video's supported promise and a rough manual candidate. Sample
across relevant sections at a cadence that fits the motion, preserving source
file, timestamp and extraction dimensions for each candidate. Closely spaced
frames may all show the same moment; retain distinct moments as well as good faces.
Do not assume a fixed cadence catches a brief product reveal.

If a visual decision model is useful and available, check its live image contract,
size limits, refusal/error behavior and price before calling it. Use the user's
chosen provider and existing authorization for footage transmission. The installed
typesafe-ai (external resource; not included) or clef (external resource; not included) can guide a
bounded image judgment; no new provider integration is required for manual work.

Supply the same title/promise and criteria to every candidate: relevant subject or
product state, clarity, composition and an expression appropriate to the content.
Allow no suitable frame. A technically clear image can still misrepresent the
video. Record model, question, candidate IDs and actual cost; a numeric score is
not a probability that the cover will win.

Inspect the shortlist visually, then extract nearby frames from the original at
full resolution. Compare them with the manual candidate at the intended crop and
small viewing size. Check natural expression, motion blur, eye line, legible
product state and the relationship to the actual payoff. Keep the selected original
frame and timestamp even if a later design adds text or a background.

When evaluating the method, use the same footage and output budget. Compare usable
distinct candidates, missed important moments, review time and total cost. Human
visual approval establishes suitability, not conversion lift.

Source: [Claire Vo's frame-picker demo](https://x.com/clairevo/status/2107600645717135503),
reviewed October 12, 2026 through local transcription and sampled frames. Around
40–68 seconds it shows candidate grids and expression, clarity, composition and
impact criteria. The source's sampling interval, API and price describe its example;
their present availability and performance were not reproduced here.
