# Sound for short product motion and UGC

Use the actual cut's timeline. For code-authored motion, derive cue times from its
events or timing function; for footage, mark the observed action. A cut does not
automatically need a sound. Select the events that need emphasis and leave space
for the foreground voice or product result.

## Worked cue mapping

Illustrative phone-demo sequence. Measure each cue time from the actual cut:

| Visible beat | Candidate sound | Check in the mixed cut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tap starts the real product action | A restrained tap, if feedback needs emphasis | Its audible onset matches contact, and it does not imply an unobserved device response. |
| The output settles into place | A soft arrival accent or silence | The sound lands on the settled result, rather than filling the whole movement. |
| A narrator explains the result | Voice in the foreground; reduce competing music | Every material word remains intelligible at ordinary playback volume. |
| The final action remains on screen | Let the cue decay; hold or reduce the score | The viewer has time to understand the action without another competing accent. |

Choose recordings, generated effects or synthesis by the brief and available
rights. Do not fill every movement with a whoosh or use a clean tone as a stand-in
for a physical material. Retain cue event/time, source and permission/license record
for each reused asset. Use separate tracks so a weak cue can be replaced locally.

For a person-led clip, direct the voice's intention, pace, pauses and emphasis for
the scene. Match recording perspective and room tone across cuts. Preserve natural
delivery where it helps understanding; do not add breaths or roughness solely to
make generated testimony appear authentic. A fictional speaker cannot establish a
real customer's experience or a product's health, financial or other claimed result.

Review the complete mix, including cue boundaries and adjacent transitions. Measure
sync, peaks, duration and delivery-format compliance separately from listening for
intelligibility, plausibility and distraction. If listening is unavailable, report
that limit; a waveform or model judgment cannot certify audible quality.

For a muted/autoplay placement, review the same cut without sound. Captions and
visible product action must carry the material promise and next action. This is a
message-comprehension check, not permission to overload every frame with text.
