## Adaptation and evidence limits

Inspect the relevant clip in playback before extracting timing, path, cadence, or sound cues. Thumbnails and contact sheets support composition and visible state changes; they do not establish smooth motion, accurate collisions, audible sound, or working controls.

The inspection retrieved all seven official example posts and their media metadata, reviewed their thumbnails, and sampled foliage, dinosaur, canopy, and physics clips. The canopy was also opened in browser playback. Original prompts, editable implementations, and complete production histories were not supplied in the retrieved posts. Code-authorship, realtime rendering, fabrication turnaround, and same-prompt model comparisons remain source claims, not independently reproduced results. Do not infer a controlled benchmark or source implementation from the recordings.

For a controlled local comparison, hold the brief, assets, starting conditions, and render settings fixed, record permitted differences and revisions, and compare synchronized output. A visually stronger example can suggest a technique without establishing model superiority.
